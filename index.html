Cube Rush – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026  |  App: Cube Rush (Android, package com.cuberush.game)

Summary

No personal data collected

Cube Rush is an offline puzzle game. It does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no advertising, no in-app purchases and no analytics or tracking tools. Everything the game stores stays on your own device.

Who we are

Cube Rush is published by RUSH GAMES (“we”, “us”). You can contact us at sa2337308@gmail.com.

Information we collect

None. We do not ask for or receive your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera data, device identifiers, advertising ID or IP address. We do not run any server that receives data from the game.

Data stored on your device

To work properly, the game keeps a few small items in the app’s private storage on your phone. This data is never sent to us or to anyone else.

ItemWhy it is stored
Your last score (a single record that is replaced after every game)To show your score on the Game Over screen and the main menu
Sound / music settingTo remember whether you muted the game
Difficulty choiceTo remember Easy, Medium or Hard
“Quick guide finished” flagSo the first-time guide is only shown once

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android Settings → Apps → Cube Rush → Storage, deletes all of this data.

Permissions

Cube Rush requests only the standard Android Vibrate permission, used for optional haptic feedback during play. It does not request access to location, contacts, storage, camera, microphone or the phone.

Audio

The music and the announcer voice clips are pre-made audio that is bundled inside the app. The game does not use your microphone and does not record any sound.

Third parties and services

  • No third-party SDKs: the game contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, social or payment libraries.
  • Android System WebView: the game screen is displayed using the Android WebView component supplied with your device. The game does not send any gameplay data through it.
  • Google Play: if you install the app from Google Play, Google handles the download and may process information about the installation under its own Privacy Policy. We do not receive personal information from Google Play.
  • Links: if you open this policy from inside the app, it opens in your web browser, which is governed by your browser’s own privacy practices.

Children

Cube Rush is a general-audience puzzle game with no chat, social features, advertising or purchases. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because nothing is collected, there is no child data to access or delete.

Data retention, security and your rights

We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to retain, disclose or sell, and nothing to access, correct or delete on our side. The small local items listed above remain on your device until you uninstall the app or clear its data, and you can remove them at any time. Because the game works offline and sends nothing off your device, the main security measure is Android’s own app sandbox. This applies regardless of where you live, including under GDPR, UK GDPR and CCPA/CPRA principles.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Cube Rush adds features that handle data (for example ads or online leaderboards), we will update this page and the “Last updated” date before that version is released.
